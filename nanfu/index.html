Pioneering the Evolution of Lasting Power

NANFU's 70-year heritage
is built upon engineering the
world's longest-lasting power

1954

Fuzhou Battery was founded, laying the foundation for what would later become NANFU Group

1988

NANFU Group was officially established, introducing China's first alkaline battery production line

1993

NANFU rose to become the leader in China's alkaline battery market, surpassing Duracell, Energizer, and all local brands

2002

Integrating multiple R&D breakthroughs, NANFU officially launched its patented Power Ring Technology

2005

NANFU joined the Procter & Gamble portfolio, adopting world-class global management and quality standards

2020

NANFU Group launched TRUSVOLT (lithium coin) and RICHPLUS (zinc-carbon) to meet the evolving needs of global consumers

2023

Introducing 4th-generation Power Ring Technology, NANFU engineered the world's longest-lasting alkaline battery
